Glimmer Quay Support

Glimmer Quay is a light-mixing puzzle game from Muhammad Ali.

How to play

Choose a red, green or blue lantern from the tray and place it in a socket along the top or left edge. Mix beams to match each home’s letter-labelled target colour. Stones block beams and nests must stay dark. The in-game How to play screen explains the rules.

Quiet Harbour and restoring purchases

The free release includes advertising. Quiet Harbour is an optional one-time purchase that removes all adverts and makes hints free. The App Store shows your local price before purchase.

If you previously purchased Quiet Harbour, open Privacy & saved data and choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

Saved progress and privacy

Progress is stored on your device. Privacy & saved data lets you erase local puzzle progress and, when required, revisit advertising privacy choices. Erasing progress cannot be undone and does not cancel purchases.
