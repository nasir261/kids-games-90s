Parcel Sprites Support

Parcel Sprites is a cozy iPhone puzzle game about guiding colourful parcels through three rotating conveyor rings.

Quick help

Choose the Inner, Middle, or Outer ring, then tap Turn Left or Turn Right. Parcels move outward automatically when transfer bays line up. Match every parcel with its coloured outer gate before the move counter reaches zero.

The question-mark button in any level reopens the tutorial. The restart button resets the current route.

Purchases
