## Description

Cozy Brick Blast is a gentle take on the classic brick-breaking arcade game. Move the paddle, keep the ball in play, and clear four colorful rows of bricks at your own pace.

Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard, then start playing immediately. Three lives keep each round friendly, while star rewards provide a simple way to track progress.

The optional Sleepy Mode gradually dims the screen, slows the game, and softens the music before ending with a calm goodnight screen.

Features:
- Simple drag controls
- Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty
- Three lives per round
- Soft original background music
- Optional Sleepy Mode
- Pause and mute controls
- Occasional advertising between completed rounds only
- Optional one-time £2.99 purchase to remove advertising permanently
- Restore Purchases support
- No accounts or registration
- Core gameplay works offline

Cozy Brick Blast is designed for relaxed play and can be enjoyed by players of all ages.

## Review notes

Cozy Brick Blast is a brick-breaking game with no login or account. Tap “Got it,” choose a difficulty, then tap “Tap to Play” to begin. Interstitial ads can appear only at natural breaks after completed rounds and never during active play. The Parent Settings screen offers a £2.99 non-consumable “Remove Ads” purchase and “Restore Purchases.” The settings passcode and game progress are stored locally and do not create an account. All music, artwork, and code are original.
