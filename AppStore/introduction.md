## Subtitle (30 chars max)

Five classic-style games in one happy app! Apple Muncher, Paddle Bounce, Brick Blast, Mole Bash & Memory Match — built for kids ages 5–6.

## Description

**The Kids Classic Games** brings together five simple, colorful games designed
just for young children (ages 5–6). No ads, no in-app purchases, no confusing
menus — just tap a game and play.

🐍 **Apple Muncher** — Steer a growing snake around the board with big arrow
buttons and munch apples to grow longer. Easy/Medium/Hard speed settings let
kids play at their own pace.

🏓 **Paddle Bounce** — Drag your paddle to bounce the ball past the computer
player. A friendly rally game that's easy to learn and hard to put down.

🧱 **Brick Blast** — Break colorful bricks with a bouncing ball and a
draggable paddle. Three lives per round keep the pressure low and the fun high.

🔨 **Mole Bash** — Tap the mole as soon as it pops out of its hole. Quick
reflexes earn stars in this 30-second timed challenge.

🃏 **Memory Match** — Flip cute animal cards and find matching pairs. Great
for building memory and concentration skills.

### Built with kids in mind
- 🎨 Bright, simple visuals and big touch targets
- ⭐ Star rewards for every game, so kids can track their progress
- 🎚️ Easy / Medium / Hard difficulty on every game
- 📖 A friendly one-time tutorial the first time each game is opened
- ⏸️ Pause anytime — perfect for snack or bathroom breaks
- 🔊 A mute button on every screen for quiet time
- 🎵 Cheerful background music throughout

### Privacy first
No accounts, no ads, no data collection, no internet connection required.
Progress (stars and difficulty) is stored only on the device.
