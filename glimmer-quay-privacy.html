Glimmer Quay Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026

Glimmer Quay does not require an account. Puzzle boards, move and hint counts, undo history, completed levels and settings are stored locally on the device so the game can resume. The developer does not receive this local game data.

Advertising

The app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google’s consent-management service for banner, interstitial and optional rewarded advertising. Depending on consent choices and region, Google and its advertising partners may process an IP-derived general location, device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, crash information and performance information to deliver, measure, secure and improve advertising. Non-personalised advertising can still involve data processing.

Where required, the app presents Google’s privacy choices before requesting ads and provides a privacy-options control in Privacy & saved data. Version 1.0 does not request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission.

In-app purchase

The optional Quiet Harbour purchase is processed by Apple. The app reads Apple-verified entitlement information to provide and restore the purchase; it does not receive payment-card details. Removing ads stops new ad requests but does not erase information previously processed by providers.

Support enquiries

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and anything you choose to include, such as device details or screenshots. This is used to investigate and reply to the request. Do not send passwords, banking information or payment-card details. Correspondence is kept only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

You can erase local progress from Privacy & saved data. Depending on your location and circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Legal exceptions may apply. Email magnetic.axis@gmail.com about information held by the developer. Provider policies explain how to make requests about information they hold.

Retention and third parties

Local game data remains until you erase it in the app or remove the app; device or cloud backups are controlled through Apple settings. Google’s retention periods depend on the data and purpose; see Google’s retention information and Google’s Privacy Policy. Apple’s handling of App Store purchases and backups is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

UK users can raise data-protection concerns with the Information Commissioner’s Office.
