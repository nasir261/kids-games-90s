HumaniWatt — Privacy Policy

Updated 12 September 2026 for the advertising and Remove Ads update

HumaniWatt is an energy-management simulation. Its Green Credits, Energy Tokens, energy prices and humanitarian projects are fictional game content, not investments, donations or real energy transactions.

Game progress

No HumaniWatt account is required. Game progress and custom project names are stored on your device. Reset Game clears your game progress; it does not cancel an Apple purchase. We do not operate a game server or upload your custom project entries.

Advertising

The free iOS app uses Google AdMob for banner adverts and optional rewarded adverts. Google’s SDK may process your IP address (including approximate location), device and app identifiers, advertising interactions, performance and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation. Our ad requests are configured for non-personalised adverts. Non-personalised adverts still involve data processing.

Google’s User Messaging Platform presents applicable consent choices before ad requests are allowed. Where available, you can revisit these through Settings → Advertising Privacy Choices. The app does not request access to Apple’s advertising identifier through App Tracking Transparency. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is a one-time, non-consumable Apple in-app purchase. It removes banner and rewarded adverts. Daily free construction boosts remain available without watching adverts. The UK price is £4.99; the app displays Apple’s price for your storefront before you purchase. Energy Token packs remain free in-game claims.

Apple processes payment and manages purchase records. We do not receive your card details. The app checks Apple-signed transactions to enable the ad-free feature. Restore Purchases in Settings checks purchases associated with your Apple Account. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Choices and retention

You can play without purchasing, decline optional rewarded adverts, and manage applicable advertising consent in the app. Purchasing Remove Ads stops further advertising requests in the app; it does not delete information already held by Google. Local game progress remains until reset or removed from the device. Google and Apple apply their own retention periods and rights processes to data they control.

Children and audience

HumaniWatt is a general-audience simulation, not a child-directed service. We do not ask users for names, contact details, precise location, camera access or contacts. Advertising is restricted to Google’s general-audience content rating. Parents can manage purchase permissions through Apple’s family and device controls.

International processing and privacy rights

Google and Apple may process information in countries outside your own as described in their policies. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal information and to complain to a data-protection authority. Contact the relevant provider about data it holds, or contact us for help with HumaniWatt’s practices.
