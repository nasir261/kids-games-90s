Frequently asked questions

  • How do I change difficulty? Open a game and use the Easy / Medium / Hard picker before you press Play.
  • What is Sleepy Mode? Tap the moon icon 🌙 in any game to turn on a gentle wind-down mode: the game slowly dims, slows down, and softens the music before ending with a "Goodnight" screen — great for bedtime.
  • How do I mute the music? Tap the speaker icon in the game screen.
  • What is the Parental Gate for? To keep young players from accidentally leaving Sleepy Mode or changing settings, a short passcode step is required. Parents can set/change the passcode from the gear ⚙️ icon.
  • I forgot the parental passcode. On the passcode screen, tap "Forgot passcode?" and answer the simple grown-up math question shown — this resets the passcode back to 1234.
  • What if I also can't remember whether I changed it? The default passcode is always 1234 unless you've changed it — try that first, or use "Forgot passcode?" to reset it.
  • The app won't open / crashes. Try deleting and reinstalling the app from the App Store. If that doesn't help, email us with your device model and iOS version.
