🌙 Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the "Cozy Sleep Time Games" pack and its standalone companion games. Cozy Sleep Time Games is listed in Apple's Kids Category for ages 5 and under. The standalone games are general-audience, family-friendly games suitable for players of all ages.

1. Information you provide

The Games do not ask you to create an account or directly provide personal information.

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical locations are collected.
  • The Games do not require or offer user registration, logins, or profiles.
  • Star ratings, difficulty settings, and the parental passcode are stored locally on your device.

2. Cozy Sleep Time Games pack

The existing Cozy Sleep Time Games pack remains a separate Kids Category app.

  • It contains no advertising.
  • It contains no in-app purchases.
  • It does not include an advertising or analytics SDK.

3. Cozy Brick Blast advertising

The standalone Cozy Brick Blast app is free and may show occasional Google AdMob ads at natural breaks between rounds. Ads are never shown during active gameplay. A one-time Apple in-app purchase can remove advertising permanently.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a general location;
  • device or advertising identifiers;
  • ads viewed and interactions with ads or the app;
  • crash logs and app performance information.

This information may be used by Google and its advertising partners to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising. Where required, Cozy Brick Blast displays Google's consent choices before requesting ads. You can revisit applicable privacy choices from Parent Settings.

See Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

4. Children's privacy

Cozy Sleep Time Games is offered in Apple's Kids Category. Cozy Brick Blast is a separate general-audience app and is not designated as Made for Kids.

  • We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information.
  • If a parent or guardian believes their child has sent us information (for example, via a support email), contact us and we will delete it promptly.

5. Purchases and outbound links

Cozy Brick Blast uses Apple's StoreKit to process the optional Remove Ads purchase. We do not receive your full payment-card details. Privacy, support, purchasing, restoration, and consent controls appear in Parent Settings behind a simple passcode gate.

6. Data retention and choices

Locally stored preferences remain on-device until you delete the app and can be cleared by deleting and reinstalling it. Advertising data is handled under Google's retention practices. You may remove ads permanently through the in-app purchase and restore that purchase on devices using the same Apple Account.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised and the new version posted at this same URL.
