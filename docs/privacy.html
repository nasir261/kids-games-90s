🌙 Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This policy applies to Cozy Sleep Time Games. The app is designed for young children and is supported by advertising, with an optional one-time in-app purchase for parents to remove advertisements.

1. Information collected by advertising services

We use Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform. According to the Google Mobile Ads SDK privacy disclosure, these services may collect:

  • Coarse location estimated from an IP address
  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising data, such as advertisements viewed
  • Product interaction data, such as app launches, taps or ad interactions
  • Crash data, performance data and other diagnostic data

This information may be used for third-party advertising, advertising or marketing, analytics and app functionality. Some information may be linked to a device. Google's SDK privacy manifest states that Device ID may be used for tracking purposes, which is reflected in the app's App Store privacy label.

2. Protections for children

Advertising requests are configured for child-directed treatment and users under the age of consent, with the maximum ad content rating set to General. Publisher first-party identifiers are disabled. The app does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission and does not intentionally request access to the IDFA.

Parents can permanently remove all advertising using the parent-gated, one-time £2.99 “Remove Ads” purchase.

3. Information stored on the device

Game progress, stars, difficulty settings, tutorials, mute settings and parental-gate settings are stored locally on the device. We do not require names, email addresses, accounts, profiles or login credentials to play.

4. Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. We do not receive payment-card or bank-account details. Purchase entitlement information is used only to unlock or restore the ad-free experience. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, Google User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before ads are requested. A privacy-options control is available in the parent-gated settings when required. Removing ads prevents future advertisements from being requested after the purchase is recognised.

6. Third-party policies and retention

Google controls the retention and processing of information collected by its advertising services. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

7. Children's privacy questions

If a parent or guardian has a privacy question or believes personal information was provided to us, please contact us. We will investigate and take appropriate action.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, advertising services or legal requirements change. The latest version will remain available at this URL.
