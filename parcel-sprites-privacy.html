Parcel Sprites Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 September 2026

Parcel Sprites does not require an account. Game progress and settings are stored locally on the device.

Advertising

The app uses Google Mobile Ads to provide banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertising. Depending on consent choices and region, Google and its advertising partners may process an IP-derived general location, device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, crash information, and performance information to deliver, measure, secure, and improve advertising.

Where required, the app presents Google’s consent choices before requesting ads. A privacy-options control is available in Settings when required. Parcel Sprites does not request App Tracking Transparency permission in version 1.0 and does not directly access the advertising identifier.

In-app purchase

The optional Quiet Depot purchase is processed by Apple. The app reads Apple-verified entitlement information to provide and restore the purchase; it does not receive payment-card details.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically to children under 13.

Your choices

You can remove automatic and banner advertising through Quiet Depot. Optional rewarded advertising is initiated only by the player. You can change available privacy choices from Settings where required by the consent platform.

Third parties

Google’s handling of information is described in Google’s Privacy Policy. Apple’s handling of App Store purchases is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, use the developer contact shown on the App Store product page. This policy may be updated when the app’s features or service providers change.
